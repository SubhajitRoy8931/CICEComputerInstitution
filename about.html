OUR STORY

From 2016 to Today

Every institute has a beginning. CICE's story is a journey from a simple idea to an established centre for practical computer education.

STORY PHOTO — ADD LATER
01 · WHERE IT BEGAN

2016

CICE Computer Institute was established in 2016 with the aim of providing accessible computer education and practical training to learners in the local community.

What started as a small initiative began with a focus on helping students develop computer skills that could be applied beyond the classroom.

02 · WHY WE STARTED

When CICE was established, the aim was to make computer education more than a classroom exercise. Learners should have the opportunity to practise what they learn, apply it in real situations, and gradually develop the confidence to use those skills independently.

This approach became part of the foundation of CICE—connecting what is taught in the classroom with what learners can actually do beyond it.

03 · GROWING ALONG THE WAY

A Journey of Growth

As the number of learners grew, so did CICE.

New courses were introduced to meet different learning needs, the computer facilities expanded, and the institute began conducting internship programs for students of colleges in the region.

Each step brought a new opportunity to improve the way students learn and practise their skills.

04 · TODAY

Where We Stand

Today, CICE Computer Institute continues to provide computer education and practical training to learners from the surrounding community.

CICE has extended its practical training beyond the classroom by conducting internship programs for students across six colleges.

With a range of courses, a dedicated computer learning environment, and years of teaching experience behind it, CICE continues to build on the purpose with which it began.

Mihir Chandra Roy is the founder and instructor of CICE Computer Institute. His involvement in computer education began before the establishment of CICE in 2016, giving him more than a decade of teaching experience.
